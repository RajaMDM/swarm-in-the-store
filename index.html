        How to read it

        1. Press Run. Shoppers enter at the bottom, visit the shelves on their list, queue and leave. Nobody tells them where the crowd is: they only see neighbouring cells.
        2. Turn on the heatmap. The hotspots you see were never coded. They emerge from thousands of local decisions.
        3. Pull a lever. Move the promo stand, close a checkout or raise arrivals. Same seed, same shoppers, different store.
        4. Break the master data. Switch to dirty data, or press Compare clean vs dirty. Watch lost items, phantom detours and walking distance move.

        Illustrative model, not calibrated to any real store. All names and data are fictitious.
